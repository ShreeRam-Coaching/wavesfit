What are cookies

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device. Similar technologies, such as local storage, do the same job. They help a site work, remember your choices, and in some cases give information to the site owner or to third parties.

How we use them

We keep this to a minimum. This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not run tracking or profiling. We only use:

  • Strictly necessary storage to remember your cookie choices. This cannot be switched off because the site could not respect your choices without it.
  • Third-party embedded content, which is off until you allow it. This is the Google Maps map in the "Where to find us" section. When it loads, Google may set cookies and receive your IP address and device information, under Google's own privacy policy.

Your choices

When you first visit, a banner asks whether to allow third-party embedded content. You can accept, decline, or choose your settings. You can change your mind at any time, and withdrawing consent is as easy as giving it:

If you decline, the site works normally. The map is replaced by a link that opens the location in Google Maps in a new tab.

Browser settings

You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Doing so may remove your saved choices here, so we may ask you again on your next visit. Instructions are available from your browser's help pages.

Changes

If we add new cookies or tools in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent where needed before they run. See also our Privacy Policy.
