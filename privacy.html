Who we are

WAVES GYM Mumbai ("WAVES GYM", "we", "us") is a gym at 5th Floor, Morya Estate, Link Road, Opposite Infiniti Mall, Veera Desai Industrial Estate, Andheri West, Mumbai, Maharashtra 400053. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and at the gym, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By using this website or joining the gym you confirm you have read it.

What we collect

When you visit this website

  • Basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, device type and pages viewed, which our hosting provider may log for security and reliability.
  • Cookie and similar data, only as described in our Cookie Policy.

This website does not have a sign-up or enquiry form. If you want to enquire, you contact us by phone, so we do not collect your details through the site itself.

When you call or visit the gym

  • Your name, phone number and any details you give us when you ask about membership or classes.

When you become a member

  • Identity and contact details (name, phone number, email, address, date of birth, emergency contact).
  • Payment and membership records (plan, dates, amounts paid). We do not store full card details; card and UPI payments are handled by payment providers.
  • Health and fitness information you choose to share, such as injuries or medical conditions relevant to training safely, and any fitness assessments. We ask for this only so we can train you safely and treat it as sensitive.
  • Check-in and attendance records, and booking records for classes.
  • CCTV footage in common areas of the gym for safety and security. Cameras are not placed in changing rooms or toilets.
  • Photos or video, only if you agree to be featured.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

We share information only where needed:

  • Service providers that help us run the gym, such as membership software, payment processors, messaging services, website hosting and IT support. They may use your data only to provide their service to us.
  • Google, if you choose to load the embedded map on our website (see our Cookie Policy).
  • Professional advisers such as accountants and lawyers.
  • Authorities where required by law, court order or to protect someone's safety.
  • A buyer or successor if the business is ever sold or reorganised, subject to this policy continuing to apply.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above. As a general guide: enquiry details for up to 12 months if you do not join; membership and payment records for the length of your membership and then for the period required by tax and accounting law; CCTV footage for a short rolling period unless needed for an incident; health information until you leave or ask us to delete it, unless we must keep it by law. When data is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable Indian law, you may have the right to:

  • Know what personal data we hold about you and how it is used.
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate data or complete incomplete data.
  • Ask us to erase data we no longer need to keep.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect what we did before you withdrew it.
  • Nominate someone to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to.
  • Have your grievance handled by us, and then approach the Data Protection Board of India.

To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to confirm who you are first.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your data, including access limited to staff who need it, password-protected systems and secure payment providers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your data, we will notify you and the authorities as the law requires.

Children

Our membership is for adults. People under 18 may train only with a parent or guardian's consent and, where we offer it, under supervision. We collect a child's information only with verifiable consent from a parent or guardian and do not use it for tracking or targeted advertising.

Transfers outside India

Some of our service providers, such as email or cloud software, may process data on servers outside India. We use providers that apply appropriate protections, and we transfer data only to countries permitted under Indian law.

Cookies

Our website uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies. See the Cookie Policy for details and to change your choices.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If we make important changes, we will tell you in a clear way, for example by a notice on this website or at reception.
